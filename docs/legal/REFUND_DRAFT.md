# Spawnt Refund Policy — draft for review

This draft is not an approved refund policy and must not be published. There are no existing customers. All local features are free; Remote and Teams are planned paid features, and their prices and purchase flows are not yet defined.

Before accepting payment, the owner must select the payment provider and state the applicable cancellation and refund process, timing, contact details, and any mandatory consumer rights. Qualified counsel should review the final policy for the markets served. Do not promise a specific refund period until it is decided.
